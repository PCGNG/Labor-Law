Demetrio L. Ellao v. Batangas I Electric Cooperative, Inc. (BATELEC I) and Raquel Rowena Rodriguez

G.R. No. 209166, July 09, 2018

Third Division, Bersamin, J.
Facts	Demetrio L. Ellao was hired by Batangas I Electric Cooperative, Inc. (BATELEC I) in 1982 and rose through the ranks to become its General Manager in 2006. In 2009, the Board of Directors of BATELEC I adopted a resolution suspending and thereafter removing Ellao from the post of General Manager on the grounds of gross inefficiency, willful neglect of duties, and loss of confidence. Ellao filed a complaint for illegal dismissal and payment of backwages, damages, and attorney's fees before the Labor Arbiter. BATELEC I filed a motion to dismiss, claiming that electric cooperatives are non-profit corporate entities governed by P.D. No. 269 and the National Electrification Administration (NEA), and that the General Manager is an officer whose removal is an intra-cooperative controversy beyond the jurisdiction of the Labor Arbiter. The Labor Arbiter, NLRC, and Court of Appeals ruled that the dispute was an intra-corporate/intra-cooperative controversy, holding that the Labor Arbiter lacked jurisdiction over the termination of the General Manager.
Issue	Whether the dismissal of a General Manager of an electric cooperative organized under P.D. No. 269 is an intra-corporate/intra-cooperative controversy falling outside the jurisdiction of the Labor Arbiter.
Held	The case is an INTRA-COOPERATIVE CONTROVERSY; the Labor Arbiter has NO JURISDICTION over the termination of an officer whose election and appointment are vested by law in the Board of Directors.


Verbatim Legal Basis:

Section 24, Presidential Decree No. 269 (National Electrification Administration Decree):

"Sec. 24. Officers. — The officers of a cooperative shall consist of a president, vice-president, secretary and treasurer, who shall be elected annually by and from the board... The board may also choose such other officers, including a general manager, as it deems necessary, who shall have such powers and perform such duties as the board may provide."


Section 5(c), Presidential Decree No. 902-A:

"Sec. 5. ... jurisdiction to hear and decide cases involving: ... (c) Controversies in the election or appointments of directors, trustees, officers or managers of such corporations, partnerships or associations."
Ratio Decidendi	1. Corporate Officer Status of General Manager: Under Section 24 of P.D. No. 269, the General Manager of an electric cooperative is not a mere employee; the position is a statutory corporate office created by the charter, whose appointment, power, compensation, and removal are vested directly in the Board of Directors.

2. Exclusion from Labor Arbiter Jurisdiction: When the position occupied is that of a corporate/cooperative officer created by law or by-laws, the controversy surrounding their removal, tenure, or compensation relates to corporate governance and is not a common labor dispute. Resolving whether Ellao was validly removed requires the interpretation of cooperative governance statutes, board resolutions, and NEA guidelines, divesting the labor tribunals of jurisdiction.
Fallo	WHEREFORE, the petition is DENIED for lack of merit. The Decision dated August 22, 2012 and the Resolution dated September 3, 2013 of the Court of Appeals in CA-G.R. SP No. 120593 are hereby AFFIRMED. Petitioner Demetrio L. Ellao's complaint is DISMISSED for lack of jurisdiction.


SO ORDERED.
