1.	Actual/pending emergencies-caused by serious accident, fire, flood typhoon, earthquake 
2.	Urgent work to be performed on the machinery/equipment to avoid serious business losses 
3.	Abnormal pressure of work and er has no other measures 
4.	Prevent loss or damage to perishable goods 
5.	Under other circumstances  
6.	Nature of work requires continuous operations and the stoppage of work may result in irreparable injury or loss to the er
