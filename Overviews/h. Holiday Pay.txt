h.	Holiday Pay (REGULAR HOLIDAY)
⮚	Except in retail and service establishments regularly employing less than 10 workers 
⮚	200% of his regular compensation 
⮚	Legal holiday - day designated or set apart by the legislature for a purpose within the meaning of the term holiday in order to commemorate an important event 
⮚	Purpose: to secure payment of undiminished monthly income undisturbed by any work interruption
⮚	Employee who does not report for work during regular holidays is paid 100%/ Employee reports for work= 200%

NOTE: SPECIAL NON-WORKING:  
o	Employee who does not report - not entitled to any compensation 
o	Employee reports = +30% 
o	If rest day also- +50%

	
RULES:  

SINGLE HOLIDAY RULE  
⮚	Employee should: 
o	a) Worked 
o	b) Was on leave with pay or 
o	c) Was on authorized absence on the day prior to the regular holiday  

SUCCESSIVE REGULAR HOLIDAYS 
⮚	To be entitled to 2 successive holidays, the employee must: 
o	a) Must be present on the day immediately preceding the 1st holiday or 
o	b) Be on leave with pay  
⮚	Otherwise, he must work on the 1st holiday to be entitled to holiday pay on the 2nd regular holiday
