TITLE: J. SERVICE CHARGES (RA 11360 & DO 242-24)
BADGE: SERVICE CHARGES / 100% DISTRIBUTION
LEGAL_BASIS: Article 96, Labor Code; RA No. 11360; DOLE D.O. No. 242-24

FACTS:
Under RA 11360 and DO 242-24, all service charges collected by hotels, restaurants, and similar establishments shall be distributed completely and equally (100%) among covered non-managerial employees.

TURNING_POINT:
The total abolition of the former 85%-15% sharing scheme in favor of 100% equal distribution to all covered rank-and-file and supervisory workers.

ISSUE:
Are managerial employees entitled to a share in collected service charges under RA 11360 and DO 242-24?

RATIONALE:
Under the express wording of Article 96 as amended by RA 11360 and DO 242-24, service charges are distributed equally among all covered employees except managerial employees.

DOCTRINE:
100% of collected service charges must be distributed equally among non-managerial employees twice a month. Withholding or pocketing these funds by managers constitutes Qualified Theft.

TAKEAWAY:
"Service charges: 100% distributed equally to all non-managerial staff. No management retention."
