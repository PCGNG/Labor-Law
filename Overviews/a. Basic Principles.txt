  X.	WAGES

Enacted in 1989, R.A. No. 6727, also known as the Wage Rationalization Act, transformed the approach to minimum wage determination.  

It established the Regional Tripartite Wages and Productivity Boards (RTWPBs) in every region. These Boards have the power to determine and fix minimum wage rates per region, industry, and sector.  

This regionalization of wage-fixing acknowledges disparities in the cost of living, economic conditions, and productivity levels across different parts of the country.  

 It provides that adjustments in minimum wage rates must be based on standards such as the needs of workers and their families, capacity of employers to pay, cost of living, and the requirements of economic and social development.

Tax Exemption for Minimum Wage Earners (Republic Act No. 9504)  
1. R.A. No. 9504, which took effect in 2008, amended the National Internal Revenue Code to exempt minimum wage earners from payment of income tax.  

2. This legislative measure is directly linked to minimum wage laws as it seeks to ensure that those earning at or near the legally mandated minimum wage enjoy the full benefit of their earnings without deduction of personal income tax.

Barangay Micro Business Enterprises (R.A. No. 9178) 
1. The Barangay Micro Business Enterprises Act of 2002 encourages the formation and growth of small community- based businesses. 

2. Qualified BMBEs may be exempted from the coverage of the minimum wage law, subject to conditions set forth by the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) and the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI). 

3. Instead of minimum wage exemptions granting carte blanche to pay arbitrary amounts, employers under BMBE arrangements are still required to provide employees with a compensation package that does not violate other labor standards, and BMBE employees remain protected against abusive labor practices.


A. WAGE DEFINED; TWIN ATTRIBUTES. 
⮚	REMUNERATION OR EARNINGS, CAPABLE OF BEINS EXPRESSED IN TERMS OF MONEY. WHETHER FIXED OR ASCERTAINED ON A TIME, TASK, PIECE OR COMMISSION BASIS OR OTHER METHOD OF CALCULATING THE SAME  
⮚	PAYABLE BY AN EMPLOYER TO AN EMPLOYEE UNDER WRITTEN OR UNWRITTEN CONTRACT OF EMPLOYMENT FOR WORK DONE OR TO BE DONE OR FOR SERVICES RENDERED OR TO BE RENDERED AND INCLUDES THE FAIR AND REASONABLE VALUE  
⮚	2 COMPONENT PARTS:  
        o	CASH WAGE - TAKES THE FORM OF READY MONEY PAID BY ER FOR SERVICES RENDERED BY THE EE.  
        o	FACILITIES - ARTICLES OR SERVICES CUSTOMARILY GIVEN FOR THE BENEFIT OF THE EE AND ARE VOLUNTARILY ACCEPTED BY HIM.  
⮚	NO WORK- NO PAY PRINCIPLE  
⮚	FAIR DAY'S WAGE FOR A FAIR DAY'S LABOR REMAINS AS THE BASIC FACTOR IN DETERMINING EMPLOYEES' WAGES AND BACK WAGES  
⮚	UNLESS EMPLOYEE WAS WILLING AND READY TO WORK BUT WAS ILLEGALLY LOCKED OUT OR SUSPENDED 

B. WAGE VERSUS SALARY; ONLY FOR PURPOSES OF ARTICLE 1708 OF THE CIVIL CODE;  
⮚	ART 1708 OF THE CIVIL CODE:  
⮚	THE LABORER'S WAGES SHALL NOT BE SUBJECT TO EXECUTION OR ATTACHMENT, EXCEPT FOR DEBTS INCURRED FOR FOOD, SHELTER, CLOTHING AND MEDICAL ATTENDANCE.  
⮚	FOR PURPOSES OF THIS ARTICLE:  
        o	WAGES - MANUAL LABOR, SKILLED OR UNSKILLED, PAID AT STATED TIMES AND MEASURED BY THE DAY, WEEK OR MONTH OR SEASON  
⮚	PAY FOR A LOWER AND LESS RESPONSIBLE CHARACTER OF EMPLOYMENT  
⮚	SALARY - HIGHER DEGREE OF EMPLOYMENT OR A SUPERIOR GRADE OF SERVICES AND IMPLIES A POSITION OF OFFICE  
⮚	SUGGESTIVE OF A LARGER AND MORE IMPORTANT SERVICE  
⮚	PURPOSE: TO FAVOR THOSE WHO ARE LABORING MEN OR WOMEN IN THE SENSE THAT THEIR WORK IS MANUAL  
        o	PERSONS BELONGING TO THIS CLASS USUALLY LOOK FORWARD TO THEIR REWARD OF A DAY'S LABOR FOR IMMEDIATE OR PRESENT SUPPORT AND SUCH PERSONS ARE MORE IN NEED OF EXEMPTION

C. FACILITIES AS PART OF WAGES; REQUIREMENTS FOR DEDUCTION  
⮚	FACILITIES - ITEMS OF EXPENSE NECESSARY FOR THE LABORER'S AND HIS FAMILY'S EXISTENCE AND SUBSISTENCE SO THAT BY EXPRESS PROVISION OF LAW THEY FORM PART OF THE WAGE AND WHEN FURNISHED BY THE ER ARE DEDUCTIBLE THEREFROM
o	  IF NOT FURNISHED, EMPLOYEE WOULD SPEND AND PAY FOR THEM JUST THE SAME  
o	DO NOT INCLUDE PROFITS - FAIR AND REASONABLE VALUE  
o	TO BE DEDUCTIBLE OR CHARGEABLE = NECESSARY THAT HE MUST RECEIVE THEM AND WRITTEN ACCEPTANCE AS VOLUNTARY  
o	CHARGED AT FAIR AND REASONABLE VALUE

D. Supplement not part of wages;
⮚	SUPPLEMENTS - EXTRA REMUNERATION OR SPECIAL PRIVILEGES OR BENEFITS GIVEN TO OR RECEIVED BY THE LABORERS OVER AND ABOVE THEIR ORDINARY EARNINGS OR WAGES  
⮚	DISTINCTION IS AS TO ITS PURPOSE  
⮚	PURPOSE TEST: IF THE BENEFIT OR PRIVILEGE GRANTED TO THE EMPLOYEE IS CLEARLY FOR THE EMPLOYER'S CONVENIENCE 
⮚	VACATION LEAVE, OT PAY IN EXCESS OF THE LEGAL RATE, PROFIT SHARING BENEFITS, SICK PENSION, RETIREMENT AND DEATH BENEFITS, CHRISTMAS BONUS

E. 13TH MONTH PAY LAW  
•	PD 851 OF 13TH MONTH PAY LAW 
•	PRO RATA ACCORDING TO THE NUMBER OF MONTHS WITHIN A YEAR THAT THE EMPLOYEE HAS RENDERED SERVICE TO THE ER  
•	ALL EMPLOYERS ARE REQUIRED TO PAY THEIR RANK-AND-FILE EMPLOYEES THE 13TH MONTH PAY NOT LATER THAN DEC 24 OF EVERY YEAR 
•	SHOULD HAVE WORKED FOR AT LEAST 1 MONTH DURING A CALENDAR YEAR  
•	ALL RANK-AND FILES IRRESPECTIVE OF THE METHODS BY WHICH THEY ARE PAID  
•	1/12 OF THE TOTAL SALARY OF AN EMPLOYEE WITHIN THE CALENDAR YEAR 
•	BASIC SALARY - INCLUDES ALL REMUNERATIONS OR EARNINGS PAID BY AN ER TO AN EE FOR SERVICES RENDERED, BUT DOES NOT INCLUDE ALLOWANCES AND MONETARY BENEFITS WHICH ARE NOT CONSIDERED EQUIVALENT OF UNUSED VACATION AND SICK LEAVE CREDITS, OT, PREMIUM, NIGHT DIFFERENTIAL AND HOLIDAY PAY.

	INCLUDES COLA  
	COMMISSION DEPENDS ON THE CIRCUMSTANCES OF THE CASE OR CONDITIONS OF PAYMENT  
	IF EARNED BY SALESMEN=FORM PART OF THEIR BASIC SALARY  
	IF MEDICAL REP = PAID AS PRODUCTIVITY BONUSES 
	DISMISSED EE ALSO ENTITLED TO THE PROPORTIONATE AMOUNT OF 13TH MONTH PAY  
	EMPLOYERS NOT COVERED BY 13TH MONTH PAY LAW: 
      o	GOVERNMENT  
      o	EMPLOYERS ALREADY PAYING THEIR EMPLOYEE BONUS OR MORE IN A CALENDAR YEAR WHICH IS AN EQUIVALENT OF 13TH MONTH PAY  
      o	KASAMBAHAY LAW ALREADY REQUIRES PAYMENT OF 13TH MONTH PAY 
      o	THOSE PAID ON PURELY COMMISSION, BOUNDARY OR TASK BASIS 
      o	THOSE PAID A FIXED AMOUNT OF PERFORMING SPECIFIC WORK
      o	SEAFARERS, MANAGERIAL EMPLOYEES

F. WHEN IS BONUS DEMANDABLE OBLIGATION 

•	BONUS - NOT DEMANDABLE ONLY WHEN MADE PART OF WORKER'S COMPENSATION  
•	IT IS A VOLUNTARY UNDERTAKING AND ENTIRELY DEPENDENT ON THE FINANCIAL CAPABILITY OF THE ER. NOT DEMANDABLE AND ENFORCEABLE OBLIGATION OF ER  
•	EXCEPTIONS: 
    o	IF IT IS GIVEN WITHOUT ANY CONDITION - PART OF THE WAGE  
    o	IF GRANT IS MANDATED BY LAW 
    o	IF IT HAS RIPENED INTO A PRACTICE THROUGH THE PASSAGE OF A CONSIDERABLE LENGTH OF TIME AND HAS CONSEQUENTLY BECOME A PART OF THE TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT OR BY VIRTUE OF ITS LONG AND REGULAR CONCESSION = IF SUFFERED BUSINESS LOSSES-NOT OBLIGED TO PAY SUCH BENEFITS  
    o	IF THE GRANT THEREOF IS THE RESULT OF AN AGREEMENT-CBA  
    o	IF GIVEN ON ACCOUNT OF COMPANY POLICY OR PRACTICE
