⮚	Every employee who has rendered at least 1 year of service (12 months of service, whether continuous or broken) shall be entitled to a yearly incentive leave of 5 days with pay 
⮚	Does not apply to those with vacation leave with pay 
⮚	Does not apply to establishments with less than 10 employees 

Purpose: to afford the laborer a chance to get a much-needed rest to replenish his worn- out energies and acquire a new vitality to enable him to efficiently perform his duties and not merely to give him additional salary 

⮚	If unused at the end of the year = convertible to cash based on the salary rate at the date of commutation 
⮚	Vacation leave is not mandatory = only thru voluntary agreement

⮚	When VL and SL are accorded=becomes a matter or right and not a mere privilege =commutation to cash can be demanded 
⮚	Pakyaw/task basis if unsupervised=not entitled to SIL  
⮚	Domestic worker = entitled but unused cannot be commuted to cash
