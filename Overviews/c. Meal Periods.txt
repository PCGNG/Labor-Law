c.	Meal Periods

⮚	GR: employees are entitled to at least 1 hour time - off for regular meals which can be taken inside or outside company premises. 
⮚	Can be freely and effectively use this hour not only for eating but also for their rest and comfort which are conducive to more efficiency and better performance in their work. 
⮚	Employer can prescribe a shorter meal period of at least 20 minutes in the ff: 
      ▪	Work is non manual in nature or does not involve strenuous physical exertion 
      ▪	Establishment regularly operates not less than 16 hours a day 
      ▪	In cases of actual or impending emergencies or there is urgent work to be performed on machineries, equip or installation to avoid serious loss which the er would otherwise suffer 
      ▪	Work is necessary to prevent serious loss of perishable goods 
⮚	Coffee breaks running from 5-20 minutes are considered as compensable working time
⮚	Rules
      ▪	Should not be less than 60 minutes 
      ▪	May be less than 60 minutes but should not be less than 20 min 
      ▪	No meal periods meals for Overtime - for short period of time only 
      ▪	Shortened meal periods at the request of the er 
      •	So employees can leave work early 
      •	Not compensable if: 
      •	EE voluntarily agrees in writing and waive OT pay 
      •	No diminution in salary and benefits 
      •	Work is not physically strenuous 
      •	Provided with coffee breaks in am and pm 
      •	OT is demandable if works beyond 430 
      •	Temporary in nature
